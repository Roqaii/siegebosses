## 01 — IMMERSEUS
**Sub:** Sha-Corrupted Elemental
**Zone:** Vale of Eternal Sorrows

**The One Rule:**
> Lose spread, kill adds, dodge wave.

**Overview:**
Two alternating phases. Phase 1 shortens each cycle as you reduce Corruption in Phase 2 — kill black puddles, heal blue ones, don't let any reach the center. Repeat until Corruption hits 0.

**Setup:** 2 Tanks · 2–3 Healers · 5–6 DPS (ranged preferred)

**Bloodlust:** On pull — first Phase 1 is the longest DPS window.

**Difficulty:** 1/5

---

### Core Mechanics

| Icon | Name | Key | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🔄 | Corrosive Blast | | Cleaving stacking armor debuff on tank. Swap immediately after every cast. |
| 💧 | Swirl | | Spinning water jet that sweeps the platform. Move out if possible. |
| 💥 | Sha Bolts | | Spread 5+ yards apart to prevent splash damage overlap. Never clump. |
| 🟦 | Split Phase — Blue Puddles | | Healers top these up. Each healed puddle cleanses Corruption from the bar. |
| ⬛ | Split Phase — Black Puddles | | DPS kills these. Every missed puddle reduces cleanse efficiency for future cycles. |

---

### Heroic Callouts

1. Swelling Corruption: boss spawns additional adds per attack — DPS urgency increases.
2. Growing Sha Pool spawns mid-fight. Assign players to step in and control its growth.

---

### Role Assignments

**Tanks:** Swap on every Corrosive Blast. AoE black puddles during split phase.

**Healers:** Spread perimeter during split. Heal ONLY blue puddles — never waste casts on Sha puddles.

**DPS:** Spread 5+ yards in Phase 1. Each DPS owns a sector during split — kill every black puddle in your zone.

---

### Strategy

**Phase 1 - Tears of the Vale**
- **All:** Group up in the same section of the room but stay 5yr spread. Do not stand in Sha puddles on the ground. Swirl rotates clockwise — you can sidestep it by moving with the rotation. When the phase ends, Sha Bolt puddles collapse to the center — move away from the middle before the transition.
- 🛡️ **Tanks:** Position to the side so Corrosive Blast does not cleave the raid. Swap at 1 stack. Off-tank picks up adds.
- ⚔️ **DPS:** Kill adds first, then focus the boss. Heroic: stagger single-target hits on Immerseus to avoid spawning too many Congealed Sha adds at once.
- 💚 **Healers:** Watch for high Swelling Corruption stacks on DPS and be ready for Swirl hitting the raid.

**Phase 2 - Split**
- **All:** Spread to pre-assigned areas of the room. Killing or healing puddles near other players grants a damage or healing buff — stay close to allies in your sector when possible.
- ⚔️ **DPS:** Kill Sha (black) adds as the primary target. Standing near an add when it dies grants a stacking damage buff.
- 💚 **Healers:** Heal Contaminated (blue) adds to full. Stay close to them to gain the Purified Residue healing and mana buff.
- 🛡️ **Tanks:** Kill adds in your area. One tank stays near Immerseus to step into the Sha Pool and reduce its size.

---

### Ability Reference

**Phase 1 - Tears of the Vale**
- **Sha Bolt** — Fires a Sha Bolt at every enemy, inflicting Shadow damage to enemies within 5 yards, and forming a Sha Pool at the targeted location.
- **Swirl** `DODGE` — Causes torrents of Sha-corrupted water to burst from beneath the ground. Players that come into contact suffer Shadow damage and are knocked away.
- **Corrosive Blast** `KEY` — Inflicts heavy Shadow damage in a cone aimed at his primary target, increasing Shadow damage taken by 200% for 45 sec. Stacks.
- **Swelling Corruption** — Gains stacks proportional to his current Corruption level. Lashes out at players who strike with single target abilities — coalescing a Congealed Sha add.

**Phase 2 - Split**
- **Sha Puddle** — When destabilized, Immerseus forms one Sha Puddle for every four units of Corruption. Destroying a Sha Puddle removes one unit of Corruption.
- **Contaminated Puddle** — Healing it to full health before it merges back makes it Purified, removing one unit of Corruption upon reaching Immerseus.
- **Erupting Sha** — When any Puddle reaches Immerseus: heavy Shadow or Frost damage to all players.

## 02 — FALLEN PROTECTORS
**Sub:** Rook · He Softfoot · Sun Tenderheart
**Zone:** Vale of Eternal Sorrows

**The One Rule:**
> All three bosses must hit 1 HP within the same 20-second window. Any boss that reaches 1 HP while another is still alive begins a heal channel — if the others don't drop in time it heals for 30% max HP.

**Overview:**
Three independent bosses that must die within the same 20-second window. Equalize their HP throughout the fight and commit only when all three are at ~10%. Recommended push order: Sun → Rook → He, then repeat.

**Setup:** 2 Tanks · 3 Healers · 5 DPS

**Bloodlust:** On pull — maximize the opening burn window before any boss hits 66%.

**Difficulty:** 2/5

---

### Core Mechanics

| Icon | Name | Key | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🛡️ | Vengeful Strikes (Rook) | | Frontal cone stun on the current tank. Face Rook away from the raid at all times — melee stands behind. Pop a personal defensive on every cast. |
| 🌀 | Clash → Spinning Crane Kick (Rook) | | Rook pulls a random target toward him, then immediately spins in AoE. Move out the moment you are pulled. |
| ☠️ | Garrote (He) | | He shadow-steps behind a random player and applies a permanent bleed that ticks for the rest of the fight — only reset by his DM phase. |
| 🔮 | Shadow Word: Bane (Sun) | | DoT on 2 random targets that jumps to 2 new targets every 3 seconds, up to 6 total. Dispel both applications within the first 3 seconds. |
| 🌊 | Calamity (Sun) | KEY | 5-second cast, deals 30% of every player's max HP to the full raid. Cover every cast with a raid cooldown. Heroic: each successive cast adds +10% damage until Sun enters DM. |
| ⚠️ | Desperate Measures (66% / 33%) | | Each boss phases individually — never trigger two simultaneously. Push order: Sun first → Rook second → He last. |

---

### Heroic Callouts

1. Each boss has an escalating passive ability that grows more dangerous the longer they go without entering a Desperate Measures phase.
2. Calamity damage escalates +10% per cast until Sun's DM resets it — a staggered raid CD rotation is mandatory.
3. He's overall damage output accounts for ~20% of total raid damage taken.
4. Rook's DM adds share HP on Heroic — stack Sorrow and Gloom in melee and cleave them together.

---

### Role Assignments

**Tank 1 — Rook:** Hold Rook in the middle for cleave facing away from the raid. Pop a defensive on every Vengeful Strikes.

**Tank 2 — He:** Never stop moving — kite He continuously through the room to prevent poison puddle accumulation.

**Healers:** 3 healers recommended. Dispel Bane within 3 seconds of application, every time. Use CDs for Calamity.

**DPS:** Push Sun first into DM, then Rook, then He — repeat the same order for the 33% cycle.

---

### Strategy

**Normal Phase — No Desperate Measures**
- **All:** Target push order: Sun reaches 66% first, then Rook, then He. Never let He fall too far behind or you'll face extended Garrote uptime with no reset at the end.
- 🛡️ **Tanks:** Rook tank: Face him away from the raid. He tank: verbally call each move direction so melee track He's path, turn your back to him when he casts Gouge.
- 💚 **Healers:** Dispel Bane within 3 seconds — coordinate so both applications are covered without duplicate dispels. Use CDs for Calamity.
- ⚔️ **DPS:** 1-2 dedicated Sun interrupters own Sha Sear. When one enters DM, full DPS stop on all bosses until adds are handled.

**Final Burn — All Bosses Sub-10%**
- **All:** Equalize all three bosses to approximately 10% HP before committing. Stack the raid, pop remaining cooldowns, and burn all three simultaneously.
- 🛡️ **Tanks:** Hold all three bosses in a tight cluster for cleave. Rook tank: angle Rook's frontal outward from the group at all times.
- 💚 **Healers:** Save one major throughput CD for this window — Calamity is at peak escalation.
- ⚔️ **DPS:** Throttle damage to keep all three within 1-2% of each other until the call to commit.

---

### Ability Reference

**Rook Stonetoe**
- **Vengeful Strikes** `KEY` — Stuns his current target and deals heavy Physical damage in a frontal cone twice per second for 3 sec. Face away from the raid always.
- **Clash → Spinning Crane Kick** `DODGE` — Pulls a random target to him then immediately spins in sustained AoE. Move out of the spin when it begins.
- **DM — Embodied Gloom** `KEY` `INTERRUPT` — Casts Corruption Shock — ~300,000 Nature damage to the target and all nearby players. Heroic #1 interrupt priority.

**Sun Tenderheart**
- **Shadow Word: Bane** `KEY` — Dispellable DoT on 2 random targets that jumps to 2 new targets every 3 seconds. Dispel both within the first 3 seconds.
- **Sha Sear** `INTERRUPT` — Channeled AoE. Heroic: ramps to ~200k on final ticks. Interrupt at the midpoint of the channel (~2.5 sec in).
- **Calamity** `KEY` — 5-second cast dealing 30% of every player's max HP. Heroic: each successive cast adds +10% damage until Sun's DM resets it.

## 03 — NORUSHEN
**Sub:** Amalgam of Corruption
**Zone:** Vale of Eternal Sorrows

**The One Rule:**
> Corruption makes you deal less damage to the boss. Cleanse through the Test Realm as fast as possible — every second you are corrupted is damage the raid is missing.

**Overview:**
A corruption-cleansing fight with a 7-minute hard enrage. Everyone starts at 75 Corruption. Enter the Purifying Light orb to take your individual trial — complete it and return cleansed. On Heroic, DPS do NOT cleanse. They stay outside and kill adds.

**Setup:** 2 Tanks · 2–3 Healers · 5–6 DPS

**Bloodlust:** On pull (Heroic). On Normal, wait until most DPS have cleansed.

**Difficulty:** 2/5

---

### Core Mechanics

| Icon | Name | Key | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| ☣️ | Corruption Bar | | Everyone starts at 75 Corruption. Higher Corruption = less damage to the boss. Click a golden Purifying Light orb to enter your private trial. |
| 🧪 | The Trials | | Tank: survive 1 minute. Healer: keep 3 NPCs alive. DPS: kill the Manifestation first, then 4 Essences while dodging bolts. |
| ⬛ | Residual Corruption Orbs | KEY | Dropped when any Unleashed Manifestation dies. Pulses 120,000 Shadow damage every 3 seconds until someone picks it up. +25 Corruption to soaker — do it immediately. |
| 🔁 | Tank Swap — Self Doubt | | Stacking debuff on the current tank — each stack increases Unleashed Anger damage by 50%. Swap at up to 3 stacks. |
| 🌀 | Blind Hatred | | Slowly rotating corruption zone that sweeps the whole room dealing 400,000 Shadow damage per second. Run away from it. |

---

### Heroic Callouts

1. Heroic DPS strategy: DPS do NOT cleanse. Stay outside, kill adds, and maintain boss uptime.
2. 7-minute hard enrage. Lust on pull.
3. Trial enemies hit significantly harder — use defensives properly inside.

---

### Role Assignments

**Tanks:** One on the boss at all times — swap at up to 3 Self Doubt stacks. Off-tank picks up all adds and soaks Residual Corruption orbs.

**Healers:** Always cleanse — the +30% healing buff makes the back half survivable. Start rotating raid CDs at ~40% as Icy Fear ramps hard.

**DPS:** Normal: follow the send order. Once cleansed, stay on the boss full-time. Heroic: do NOT cleanse. Stay outside, kill adds, attack the boss.

---

### Strategy

**Phase 1 — Main Realm & Trial Rotation**
- **All:** The send rotation is the entire fight. Do NOT touch Orbs of Corruption inside the trial — they add Corruption and cancel your cleanse.
- 🛡️ **Tanks:** One tank holds the boss at all times. Off-tank picks up all adds from completed trials. Only enter the Trial Realm during a clean swap window.
- 💚 **Healers:** Always cleanse. Never leave zero healers outside simultaneously. Track Blind Hatred.
- ⚔️ **DPS:** Normal: follow send order. Once cleansed, go full-time on the boss. Heroic: do NOT cleanse. Kill adds and maintain boss uptime.

**Sub-50% — Escalation & Burn**
- **All:** At 50% and every 10% after, the boss spawns an extra Manifestation. Icy Fear ramps raidwide damage. Stay on the boss.
- 💚 **Healers:** Rotate major raid CDs from ~40% onward. Do not hold them.
- ⚔️ **DPS:** Stay on the boss. Only swap to Manifestations if called.

---

### Ability Reference

**Amalgam of Corruption**
- **Self Doubt** `KEY` — Stacking debuff on the current tank. Each stack increases Unleashed Anger damage by 50%. Swap at up to 3 stacks.
- **Blind Hatred** `KEY` `DODGE` — A corruption zone that slowly rotates. Deals 400,000 Shadow damage per second to anyone in its path.
- **Fusion** `KEY` — Each Expelled Corruption bolt that reaches the Amalgam increases its damage by 8%. Kill all Essences before they reach the boss.
- **Icy Fear** — Constant frost damage to all players — increases as the Amalgam loses health. Soft healing enrage from ~40%.

## 04 — SHA OF PRIDE
**Sub:** Pride Bar Management · Heroic: Rifts + Banishment
**Zone:** Vale of Eternal Sorrows

**The One Rule:**
> Never reach 100 Pride — it permanently mind controls you. Dispel Mark of Arrogance only while Gift of the Titans is active, and close Rifts of Corruption before they overwhelm the room.

**Overview:**
A pride management fight with two heroic-only additions: Rifts of Corruption and Banishment. Keep Pride under 50 at all times, dispel only during Gift windows, and close rifts in your assigned zone. At 30% all Pride resets — burn hard.

**Setup:** 2 Tanks · 2 Healers · 6 DPS

**Bloodlust:** At the 30% transition (Unleashed) — all Pride resets, boss kills Norushen, escalating damage begins every 10 seconds.

**Difficulty:** 2/5

---

### Core Mechanics

| Icon | Name | Key | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🌀 | Swelling Pride | KEY | Raid-wide damage + 5 Pride to all. Secondary effect based on current Pride: 25–49: ground marker. 50–74: run to shadow. 75–99: spread. 100: mind controlled permanently. |
| 😤 | Mark of Arrogance | KEY | DoT on 2 players for the rest of the fight. Only dispel while Gift of the Titans is active. |
| 🔒 | Corrupted Prison | KEY | 2 non-tank players imprisoned. +5 Pride per second until freed. Two players stand on orange lock runes simultaneously. |
| 👻 | Manifestation of Pride | | Spawns on the west wall. Interrupt Mocking Blast. Kill with ranged. |
| 🔴 | Rifts of Corruption (Heroic) | KEY | Spawns every 8 seconds — fires 350k + 5 Pride bolts. Close rifts by running over them (1-minute debuff after). |
| 🌀 | Banishment (Heroic) | KEY | One non-tank teleported into a Pac-Man maze. Auto-runs, collect orbs, avoid minions. All DPS switch to Corrupted Fragment. |

---

### Heroic Callouts

1. Rifts of Corruption spawn every 8 seconds and fill the room if ignored. Assign each player a default zone.
2. Banishment sends one non-tank into a maze every ~75 seconds. All DPS switch to the Fragment immediately.
3. Run 2 healers. HPS requirements are among the lowest in the zone.
4. Gift of the Titans is the only window to dispel Mark of Arrogance.

---

### Role Assignments

**Tanks:** Swap immediately on Wounded Pride. Park the boss center-room for rift access. Cover your assigned prison lock on every spawn.

**Healers:** Dispel Mark of Arrogance only while Gift of the Titans is active. Stack within 8 yards of Gift carriers for Power of the Titans.

**DPS:** Own a rift zone — close yours on sight. 1–2 melee assigned to interrupt Manifestation of Pride every spawn. All DPS switch to Fragment on Banishment call.

---

### Strategy

**Phase 1 — Pride Management (100% to 30%)**
- **All:** Position in a loose ring around the boss, spread enough that rift bolts don't hit adjacent players. Keep Pride under 50 at all times. Return to default positions between mechanics.
- 🛡️ **Tanks:** Swap immediately on Wounded Pride application. Both tanks cover their assigned prison locks.
- 💚 **Healers:** Watch for Gift of the Titans on raid frames. Stack within 8 yards of the other Gift carriers for Power of the Titans.
- ⚔️ **DPS:** Stay in your default position zone. When a rift spawns near you, close it immediately. Call "have debuff" on voice after closing.

**Sub-30% — Unleashed Burn**
- **All:** At 30%, all Pride resets to 0. Pop Bloodlust and every offensive cooldown. Kill before the next Swelling Pride cycle.
- 💚 **Healers:** Dispel Mark of Arrogance freely now — no Gift requirement. Assign a major throughput cooldown for the Unleashed ticks.
- ⚔️ **DPS:** Full cooldowns. Continue closing rifts. Don't die to a rift bolt when you're one minute from a kill.

---

### Ability Reference

**Pride — Core Mechanic**
- **Swelling Pride** `KEY` — Cast when boss reaches 100 energy. Deals ~522k Shadow damage and +5 Pride. Secondary effects escalate: Bursting Pride (25–49), Projection (50–74), Aura of Pride (75–99), Overcome (100 = permanent MC).
- **Mark of Arrogance** `KEY` — DoT on 2 random players dealing ~110k Shadow per tick for the rest of the fight. Only dispel with Gift of the Titans active.
- **Corrupted Prison** `KEY` — Two non-tank players imprisoned, taking ~108k Shadow per second and +5 Pride per second. Release requires two players on the two lock runes simultaneously.

**Heroic-Only Abilities**
- **Rifts of Corruption** `KEY` — Every 8 seconds a rift spawns and fires bolts — 350k Shadow + 5 Pride. Close by running over it (applies 60-second debuff). Prioritize rifts near the raid.
- **Banishment** `KEY` — Random non-tank teleported into Sha Realm. Auto-runs at +50% speed — steer with mouse-look. Avoid instant-death minions. Collect orbs (+25% damage to Fragment). Return when Fragment dies.

## 05 — GALAKRAS
**Sub:** Tower + Ground Split Fight
**Zone:** Gates of Retribution

**The One Rule:**
> Tower speed controls the entire fight. A slow tower team means more Drake Fire stacks on the ground team — every second the tower drags is more unavoidable spike damage below.

**Overview:**
Phase 1 is an extended split-team add gauntlet: ground team defends NPC faction leaders while tower team clears two towers to unlock anti-air turrets. Shooting Galakras with both turrets triggers Phase 2 — a straightforward burn fight.

**Setup:** 2 Tanks · 2–3 Healers · 5–6 DPS

**Bloodlust:** On pull in Phase 2 (or hold for ~40% if Pulsing Flames is escalating hard).

**Difficulty:** 2/5

---

### Core Mechanics

| Icon | Name | Key | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🔥 | Drake Fire | | Proto-drakes target random ground players for ~150k fire damage to anyone within 5 yards, climbing 10% every cast. Spread 5+ yards at all times. |
| 🧨 | Healing Tide Totem | KEY | Tidal Shamans drop a totem healing nearby enemies 10–20% HP every 3 seconds. Kill it instantly on spawn — #1 ground priority every wave. |
| 🛡️ | Bonecrusher — Fracture | KEY | Charges a faction leader NPC and deals 10% of their HP per second. Stun it the instant it charges — leader death resets the encounter. |
| ⚔️ | Crusher's Call | | Thranok grips 5 players to him then immediately follows with a 1M+ AoE stomp. Move out the moment you land. |
| 🌊 | Flames of Galakrond | | Orb targets a random player — explodes for up to 900k but reduced by each player it passes through. Targeted player steps behind the raid cluster. |

---

### Heroic Callouts

1. Demolitionists: friendly NPCs that open the towers are attacked by enemy Grunts. Assign 1 DPS + 1 healer to protect them.
2. Drake Fire ramps hard — stagger healer and DPS defensive cooldowns across Phase 1.
3. Add waves overlap because mobs have significantly more HP.

---

### Role Assignments

**Tower Tank:** Kite Korgra off Poison Clouds. Stay to the side of Krugruk and never stand in his cone. Fire turret on call.

**Ground Tank:** Call every Bonecrusher charge on comms so stuns are ready. Keep Thranok away from the raid for Shattering Strike.

**Healers:** Split one per team. Ground healer staggers Drake Fire CDs. Both healers full throughput for Phase 2.

**DPS:** Ground: kill priority — Totem > Shaman > Bonecrusher > Flagbearer. Stay spread 5+ yards. Tower: train mobs to the top and cleave.

---

### Strategy

**Phase 1 — Ground Team**
- **All:** Stay at or behind the front of the NPC line. Spread 5+ yards at all times. Kill priority every wave: Healing Tide Totem → Tidal Shaman → Bonecrusher → Flagbearer → everything else.
- 🛡️ **Ground Tank:** Face Thranok away from the raid. Hold Bonecrushers on aggro but verbally call every charge so the raid has stuns ready.
- ⚔️ **DPS:** Kill priority without exception. Never deviate. Two waves overlapping — split interrupts explicitly.

**Phase 2 — Flames of Galakrond Positioning**
- **All:** Split the raid into two groups behind the boss, forming a triangle. Use a strict every-3-casts alternating rotation for Flames of Galakrond. The 15-second DoT expires in exactly 3 casts, so this rotation automatically caps stacks.
- ⚔️ **DPS:** The moment you are targeted, run directly to whichever extension marker is active for this set of 3 casts. Return to your group position immediately after the orb explodes.

---

### Ability Reference

**Phase 1 — Ground Adds**
- **Healing Tide Totem** `KEY` — Dropped by Tidal Shaman. Heals nearby enemies for 10–20% max HP every 3 seconds. #1 kill target — very low HP.
- **Bonecrusher — Fracture** `KEY` — Charges a faction leader NPC, dealing 10% of their HP per second. Interrupt Fracture by stunning the Bonecrusher instantly.
- **Flames of Galakrond (Phase 2)** `KEY` `DODGE` — Orb fired at a random player. Explodes for up to 900k reduced by each player it passes through. Targeted player steps behind the raid.

## 06 — IRON JUGGERNAUT
**Sub:** Assault and Siege Phases Alternate
**Zone:** Gates of Retribution

**The One Rule:**
> Never let all three Crawler Mines detonate freely. Assign your soakers before the pull — on Heroic each stomp hits for ~1.5M physical.

**Overview:**
Two alternating phases on a roughly 2-minute Assault / 1-minute Siege loop. Assault Phase is the only DPS window. The recommended Siege strategy is to let Shock Pulse knock the entire raid into the far corner, out-ranging Mortar Barrage.

**Setup:** 2 Tanks · 3 Healers · 5 DPS

**Bloodlust:** On pull — the first Assault Phase is the longest uninterrupted DPS window of the fight.

**Difficulty:** 2/5

---

### Core Mechanics

| Icon | Name | Key | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🔥 | Flame Vents | | Frontal cone on the current tank. Applies a stacking fire DoT (Ignite Armor). Swap at 2 stacks. |
| 💣 | Crawler Mines | KEY | Three mines spawn every ~30 seconds and arm after 15 seconds. Off-tank soaks two per set; pre-assigned DPS backup handles the third. On Heroic each stomp hits ~1.5M physical. |
| 🌀 | Ricochet (Heroic) | | A sawblade arcs in a triangle between two ranged players. Sidestep the moment you see it launch. |
| 💥 | Shock Pulse — Siege | KEY | Knocks the entire raid ~60–80 yards. Stack in melee range just before the cast so everyone lands in the far corner together. |
| 🔴 | Cutter Laser — Siege | | Chases a targeted player. Kite along the outer wall. Never cross an Explosive Tar puddle. |

---

### Heroic Callouts

1. Ricochet: spinning sawblade arcs toward two ranged players every ~15 seconds.
2. Mortar Barrage: replaces Mortar Cannon in Siege Phase. Out-range it by letting Shock Pulse knock you to the far corner.
3. Crawler Mine stomps hit ~1.5M physical — soakers need a cooldown every time.

---

### Role Assignments

**Tanks:** Swap at 2 Flame Vents stacks. Off-tank is primary mine soaker. Both tanks stack with the raid before every Shock Pulse.

**Healers:** 3 healers required. Assign a raid cooldown to each of the three Shock Pulses before the pull.

**DPS:** Spread ranged loosely for Ricochet — sidestep at launch. Pre-assign one backup mine soaker with a personal cooldown.

---

### Strategy

**Assault Phase**
- **All:** Stay loosely spread — Borer Drill cracks ripple toward player positions and Ricochet arcs between two ranged. This is the only phase where you damage the boss.
- 🛡️ **Tanks:** Swap at 2 Flame Vents stacks. Off-tank immediately moves to cover the next mine set after dropping aggro.
- ⚔️ **DPS:** Spread ranged loosely. Backup mine soaker watches the off-tank — if their cooldowns are spent, step in on the third mine.

**Siege Phase — Shock Pulse Strategy**
- **All:** Stack the entire raid in melee range just before Shock Pulse casts — the knockback sends everyone to the far corner together. Stay in that corner for the entire Siege Phase. At this range Mortar Barrage cannot reach you.
- 🛡️ **Tanks:** Both tanks stack with the raid before Shock Pulse. Pre-assign the three Shock Pulse cooldowns: CD1 first Pulse, CD2 second, CD3 third.
- ⚔️ **DPS:** Once in the corner your job is mine management and laser kiting only. If Cutter Laser targets you, kite along the wall away from tar patches.

---

### Ability Reference

**Assault Mode**
- **Flame Vents** `KEY` — Frontal cone dealing heavy Fire damage to the current tank. Applies Ignite Armor: +10% Fire damage taken. Stacks. Swap at 2 stacks.
- **Crawler Mines** `KEY` — Three mines spawn, arm after 15 seconds. All three detonating freely is near-fatal. Stomp a mine: deals ~1.5M Physical damage (armor-reduced) to the soaker. Dedicated cooldown required on Heroic.
- **Ricochet** `KEY` — Heroic only. Spinning sawblade arcs in a triangle toward two ranged players. Sidestep two or three steps when it fires — blade targets your position at launch.

**Siege Mode**
- **Shock Pulse** `KEY` — Knocks all players back ~60–80 yards on Heroic. Three occur per Siege Phase. Stack in melee range before the cast — everyone lands together in the far corner, out of Mortar Barrage range.
- **Mortar Barrage** `KEY` — Heroic only. Fires six to eight rapid mortar blasts at player locations within ~60 yards. Avoided entirely by staying out of range from the far corner.
- **Cutter Laser** — Tail cannon locks onto a random player and chases them. Kite along the outer wall. Never run through Explosive Tar — laser contact triggers a ~500k raid-wide explosion.

## 07 — KOR'KRON DARK SHAMAN
**Sub:** Haromm and Kardris — Heroic Split Strategy
**Zone:** Gates of Retribution

**The One Rule:**

### Strategy

**Phase 1 — The True Horde (100% to 10%)**
- **All:** Stack under the boss. 3 designated range players always stand at distance to bait Desecrate away from the raid. Wait for Warbringers to reach the center of the room before engaging.
- 🛡️ **Tanks:** Let all Warbringers reach the center then pick them all up at once. First wave: AoE before War Song. Second wave: drop to ~30-50% then use knockbacks/CC to hold them in the Iron Star's path.
- ⚔️ **DPS:** Kill Siege Engineers fast — 10-second window. First engineer: send highest-burst DPS solo. Second engineer: send 2 DPS.

**Intermission #1 — Jade Temple**
- **All:** 15 Embodied Doubt adds in 5 groups of 3. Pre-assigned pairs own each group. Kill all adds before Garrosh hits 25 energy (16-second window). Interrupt Embodied Doubt every cast — 3+ casts is a wipe.
- ⚔️ **DPS:** Know your pair, your group, and your interrupt rotation before the pull. Do not improvise. Once your group is dead, sprint to assist the middle group.

**Intermission #2 — Terrace (SKIP)**
- **All:** Run to the back wall immediately and stand there. Wait out the full 60-second timer. Skipping eliminates execution risk with no meaningful downside.

**Phase 2 — Power of Y'Shaarj**
- **All:** Stack under the boss except during Empowered Whirling Corruption (spread to 40 yards) and Desecrate weapon placement. Kill each Minion of Y'Shaarj separately — never let two die adjacent to each other.
- 🛡️ **Tanks:** Swap at 3 Gripping Despair stacks. Hold boss stationary in a fixed corner so the Desecrate team can predict placement zones.
- ⚔️ **DPS:** Interrupt the MC target's spread cast every single time. Do not use DoTs that persist and kill the MC'd player before they hit 20% HP.

**Phase 3 — My World (10% to transition)**
- **All:** All abilities now empowered. Garrosh heals to full. Pop Bloodlust immediately. Mind Controls are highest priority. When boss hits 0%, do NOT attack — 20-second RP before Phase 4 teleport.
- 🛡️ **Tanks:** When Gripping Despair stacks fall off in Phase 3, use a defensive — Explosive Despair hits harder and increases by 10% per previous explosion.

**Phase 4 — Stormwind (Heroic Only)**
- **All:** Drag the boss to one edge. Raid stacks just inside the Malice circle edge. When you get Malicious Blast debuff, step outside for 4 seconds, return. Kill the boss before Manifest Rage fires twice.
- 🛡️ **Off-Tank:** Kite the Iron Star along the wall and route it to intercept Garrosh just as he reaches 100 energy and starts casting Manifest Rage. Hit during the 2-second cast — not the channel.
- ⚔️ **DPS:** Stay stacked behind the boss inside the Malice circle. For Malice #4 + Bombardment overlap: 3 pre-assigned players with large defensives soak together while rest spreads.

### Ability Reference

**Phase 1**
- **Desecrate** — Thrown at a ranged target. 3 players always at range to bait it. Place weapons along walls. In Phase 2+ weapons are unkillable.
- **Siege Engineer** `KEY` — Spawns on balcony. 10-second window to kill before Iron Star activates uncontrolled. Heroic: 3M HP — send highest burst DPS solo for the first.

**Phase 2 / 3**
- **Whirling Corruption** `KEY` — Stack for base version. Empowered: spread to 40 yards — missiles spawn Minion adds at landing positions. Kill each minion separately.
- **Touch of Y'Shaarj** `KEY` — MC on 2 players who spread the MC on cast. Interrupt or stun every cast. DPS to 20% HP to break. No DoTs that can kill the player.
- **Gripping Despair → Explosive Despair (P3)** `KEY` — Stacking tank DoT. Swap at 3. In Phase 3 dropping stacks triggers Explosive Despair — use a tank defensive every time stacks fall.

**Intermission**
- **Embodied Doubt (Jade Temple)** `KEY` — AoE silence + stacking damage amplifier. Pre-assign interrupt/stun pairs. Kill all 15 adds before Garrosh hits 25 energy.

**Phase 4 (Heroic Only)**
- **Malice** `KEY` — Raid stacks at circle edge. Step outside on Malicious Blast debuff, return after 4 seconds. If fewer than 2 inside, Garrosh gains energy.
- **Manifest Rage** `KEY` — Cast at 100 energy. Iron Star must interrupt during the 2-second cast window. Each second of channel spawns more adds.
- **Iron Star (Phase 4)** `KEY` — Spawned by Bombardment clump check. Off-tank kites along wall to intercept Garrosh at exactly 100 energy.
