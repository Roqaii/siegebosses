## BOSS 01 — IMMERSEUS
**Page:** /bosses/immerseus
**Sub (hero tagline):** Sha-Corrupted Elemental
**Setup:** 2 Tanks · 2-3 Healers · 5-6 ranged preferred DPS

**Rule (THE ONE RULE block):**
Lose spread, kill adds, dodge wave.

**Overview (Fight Overview section):**
Two alternating phases. Phase 1 shortens each cycle as you reduce Corruption in Phase 2 — kill black puddles, heal blue ones, don't let any reach the center. Repeat until Corruption hits 0.

**Lust (Bloodlust banner):**
On pull — first Phase 1 is the longest DPS window.

**Heroic Callouts:**
1. Swelling Corruption: boss spawns additional adds per attack — DPS urgency increases.
2. Growing Sha Pool spawns mid-fight. Assign players to step in and control its growth.

**Mechanics:**
1. [🔄] Corrosive Blast — Cleaving stacking armor debuff on tank. Swap immediately after every cast.
2. [💧] Swirl — Spinning water jet that sweeps the platform. Move out if possible.
3. [💥] Sha Bolts — Spread 5+ yards apart to prevent splash damage overlap. Never clump.
4. [🟦] Split Phase — Blue Puddles — Healers top these up. Each healed puddle cleanses Corruption from the bar.
5. [⬛] Split Phase — Black Puddles — DPS kills these. Every missed puddle reduces cleanse efficiency for future cycles.

**Roles:**
- Tanks: Swap on every Corrosive Blast. AoE black puddles during split phase.
- Healers: Spread perimeter during split. Heal ONLY blue puddles — never waste casts on Sha puddles.
- DPS: Spread 5+ yards in Phase 1. Each DPS owns a sector during split — kill every black puddle in your zone.

**Phases (accordion):**
Phase 1 - Tears of the Vale
  All: Group up in the same section of the room but stay 5yr spread. Do not stand in Sha puddles on the ground. Swirl rotates clockwise — you can sidestep it by moving with the rotation. When the phase ends, Sha Bolt puddles collapse to the center — move away from the middle before the transition.
  Tanks: Position to the side so Corrosive Blast does not cleave the raid. Swap at 1 stack. Off-tank picks up adds.
  DPS: Kill adds first, then focus the boss. Heroic: stagger single-target hits on Immerseus to avoid spawning too many Congealed Sha adds at once.
  Healers: Watch for high Swelling Corruption stacks on DPS and be ready for Swirl hitting the raid.

Phase 2 - Split
  All: Spread to pre-assigned areas of the room. Killing or healing puddles near other players grants a damage or healing buff — stay close to allies in your sector when possible.
  DPS: Kill Sha (black) adds as the primary target. Standing near an add when it dies grants a stacking damage buff.
  Healers: Heal Contaminated (blue) adds to full. Stay close to them to gain the Purified Residue healing and mana buff.
  Tanks: Kill adds in your area. One tank stays near Immerseus to step into the Sha Pool and reduce its size.

## BOSS 02 — FALLEN PROTECTORS
**Page:** /bosses/fallen-protectors
**Sub:** Rook · He Softfoot · Sun Tenderheart
**Setup:** 2 Tanks · 3 Healers · 5 DPS

**Rule:**
All three bosses must hit 1 HP within the same 20-second window. Any boss that reaches 1 HP while another is still alive begins a heal channel — if the others don't drop in time it heals for 30% max HP.

**Overview:**
Three independent bosses that must die within the same 20-second window. Equalize their HP throughout the fight and commit only when all three are at ~10%. Recommended push order: Sun → Rook → He, then repeat.

**Lust:**
On pull — maximize the opening burn window before any boss hits 66%.

**Heroic Callouts:**
1. Each boss has an escalating passive ability that grows more dangerous the longer they go without entering a Desperate Measures phase.
2. Calamity damage escalates +10% per cast until Sun's DM resets it — a staggered raid CD rotation is mandatory.
3. He's overall damage output accounts for ~20% of total raid damage taken.
4. Rook's DM adds share HP on Heroic — stack Sorrow and Gloom in melee and cleave them together.

**Mechanics:**
1. [🛡️] Vengeful Strikes (Rook) — Frontal cone stun on the current tank. Face Rook away from the raid at all times — melee stands behind. Pop a personal defensive on every cast.
2. [🌀] Clash → Spinning Crane Kick (Rook) — Rook pulls a random target toward him, then immediately spins in AoE. Move out the moment you are pulled.
3. [☠️] Garrote (He) — He shadow-steps behind a random player and applies a permanent bleed that ticks for the rest of the fight — only reset by his DM phase.
4. [🔮] Shadow Word: Bane (Sun) — DoT on 2 random targets that jumps to 2 new targets every 3 seconds, up to 6 total. Dispel both applications within the first 3 seconds.
5. [🌊] Calamity (Sun) [KEY] — 5-second cast, deals 30% of every player's max HP to the full raid. Cover every cast with a raid cooldown. Heroic: each successive cast adds +10% damage until Sun enters DM.
6. [⚠️] Desperate Measures (66% / 33%) — Each boss phases individually — never trigger two simultaneously. Push order: Sun first → Rook second → He last.

**Roles:**
- Tank 1 — Rook: Hold Rook in the middle for cleave facing away from the raid. Pop a defensive on every Vengeful Strikes.
- Tank 2 — He: Never stop moving — kite He continuously through the room to prevent poison puddle accumulation.
- Healers: 3 healers recommended. Dispel Bane within 3 seconds of application, every time. Use CDs for Calamity.
- DPS: Push Sun first into DM, then Rook, then He — repeat the same order for the 33% cycle.

**Phases:**
Normal Phase — No Desperate Measures
  All: Target push order: Sun reaches 66% first, then Rook, then He. Never let He fall too far behind or you'll face extended Garrote uptime with no reset at the end.
  Tanks: Rook tank: Face him away from the raid. He tank: verbally call each move direction so melee track He's path, turn your back to him when he casts Gouge.
  Healers: Dispel Bane within 3 seconds — coordinate so both applications are covered without duplicate dispels. Use CDs for Calamity.
  DPS: 1-2 dedicated Sun interrupters own Sha Sear. When one enters DM, full DPS stop on all bosses until adds are handled.

Final Burn — All Bosses Sub-10%
  All: Equalize all three bosses to approximately 10% HP before committing. Stack the raid, pop remaining cooldowns, and burn all three simultaneously.
  Tanks: Hold all three bosses in a tight cluster for cleave. Rook tank: angle Rook's frontal outward from the group at all times.
  Healers: Save one major throughput CD for this window — Calamity is at peak escalation.
  DPS: Throttle damage to keep all three within 1-2% of each other until the call to commit.

## BOSS 03 — NORUSHEN
**Page:** /bosses/norushen
**Sub:** Amalgam of Corruption
**Setup:** 2 Tanks · 2-3 Healers · 5-6 DPS

**Rule:**
Corruption makes you deal less damage to the boss. Cleanse through the Test Realm as fast as possible — every second you are corrupted is damage the raid is missing.

**Overview:**
A corruption-cleansing fight with a 7-minute hard enrage. Everyone starts at 75 Corruption. Enter the Purifying Light orb to take your individual trial — complete it and return cleansed. On Heroic, DPS do NOT cleanse. They stay outside and kill adds.

**Lust:**
On pull (Heroic). On Normal, wait until most DPS have cleansed.

**Heroic Callouts:**
1. Heroic DPS strategy: DPS do NOT cleanse. Stay outside, kill adds, and maintain boss uptime.
2. 7-minute hard enrage. Lust on pull.
3. Trial enemies hit significantly harder — use defensives properly inside.

**Mechanics:**
1. [☣️] Corruption Bar — Everyone starts at 75 Corruption. Higher Corruption = less damage to the boss. Click a golden Purifying Light orb to enter your private trial.
2. [🧪] The Trials — Tank: survive 1 minute. Healer: keep 3 NPCs alive. DPS: kill the Manifestation first, then 4 Essences while dodging bolts.
3. [⬛] Residual Corruption Orbs [KEY] — Dropped when any Unleashed Manifestation dies. Pulses 120,000 Shadow damage every 3 seconds until someone picks it up. +25 Corruption to soaker — do it immediately.
4. [🔁] Tank Swap — Self Doubt — Stacking debuff on the current tank — each stack increases Unleashed Anger damage by 50%. Swap at up to 3 stacks.
5. [🌀] Blind Hatred — Slowly rotating corruption zone that sweeps the whole room dealing 400,000 Shadow damage per second. Run away from it.

**Roles:**
- Tanks: One on the boss at all times — swap at up to 3 Self Doubt stacks. Off-tank picks up all adds and soaks Residual Corruption orbs.
- Healers: Always cleanse — the +30% healing buff makes the back half survivable. Start rotating raid CDs at ~40% as Icy Fear ramps hard.
- DPS: Normal: follow the send order. Once cleansed, stay on the boss full-time. Heroic: do NOT cleanse. Stay outside, kill adds, attack the boss.

## BOSS 04 — SHA OF PRIDE
**Page:** /bosses/sha-of-pride
**Sub:** Pride Bar Management · Heroic: Rifts + Banishment
**Setup:** 2 Tanks · 2 Healers · 6 DPS

**Rule:**
Never reach 100 Pride — it permanently mind controls you. Dispel Mark of Arrogance only while Gift of the Titans is active, and close Rifts of Corruption before they overwhelm the room.

**Overview:**
A pride management fight with two heroic-only additions: Rifts of Corruption and Banishment. Keep Pride under 50 at all times, dispel only during Gift windows, and close rifts in your assigned zone. At 30% all Pride resets — burn hard.

**Lust:**
At the 30% transition (Unleashed) — all Pride resets, boss kills Norushen, escalating damage begins every 10 seconds.

**Heroic Callouts:**
1. Rifts of Corruption spawn every 8 seconds and fill the room if ignored. Assign each player a default zone.
2. Banishment sends one non-tank into a maze every ~75 seconds. All DPS switch to the Fragment immediately.
3. Run 2 healers. HPS requirements are among the lowest in the zone.
4. Gift of the Titans is the only window to dispel Mark of Arrogance.

**Mechanics:**
1. [🌀] Swelling Pride [KEY] — Raid-wide damage + 5 Pride to all. Secondary effect based on current Pride: 25–49: ground marker. 50–74: run to shadow. 75–99: spread. 100: mind controlled permanently.
2. [😤] Mark of Arrogance [KEY] — DoT on 2 players for the rest of the fight. Only dispel while Gift of the Titans is active.
3. [🔒] Corrupted Prison [KEY] — 2 non-tank players imprisoned. +5 Pride per second until freed. Two players stand on orange lock runes simultaneously.
4. [👻] Manifestation of Pride — Spawns on the west wall. Interrupt Mocking Blast. Kill with ranged.
5. [🔴] Rifts of Corruption (Heroic) [KEY] — Spawns every 8 seconds — fires 350k + 5 Pride bolts. Close rifts by running over them (1-minute debuff after).
6. [🌀] Banishment (Heroic) [KEY] — One non-tank teleported into a Pac-Man maze. Auto-runs, collect orbs, avoid minions. All DPS switch to Corrupted Fragment.

**Roles:**
- Tanks: Swap immediately on Wounded Pride. Park the boss center-room for rift access. Cover your assigned prison lock on every spawn.
- Healers: Dispel Mark of Arrogance only while Gift of the Titans is active. Stack within 8 yards of Gift carriers for Power of the Titans.
- DPS: Own a rift zone — close yours on sight. 1–2 melee assigned to interrupt Manifestation of Pride every spawn. All DPS switch to Fragment on Banishment call.

## BOSS 05 — GALAKRAS
**Page:** /bosses/galakras
**Sub:** Tower + Ground Split Fight
**Setup:** 2 Tanks · 2-3 Healers · 5-6 DPS

**Rule:**
Tower speed controls the entire fight. A slow tower team means more Drake Fire stacks on the ground team — every second the tower drags is more unavoidable spike damage below.

**Overview:**
Phase 1 is an extended split-team add gauntlet: ground team defends NPC faction leaders while tower team clears two towers to unlock anti-air turrets. Shooting Galakras with both turrets triggers Phase 2 — a straightforward burn fight.

**Lust:**
On pull in Phase 2 (or hold for ~40% if Pulsing Flames is escalating hard).

**Heroic Callouts:**
1. Demolitionists: friendly NPCs that open the towers are attacked by enemy Grunts. Assign 1 DPS + 1 healer to protect them.
2. Drake Fire ramps hard — stagger healer and DPS defensive cooldowns across Phase 1.
3. Add waves overlap because mobs have significantly more HP.

## BOSS 06 — IRON JUGGERNAUT
**Page:** /bosses/iron-juggernaut
**Sub:** Assault and Siege Phases Alternate
**Setup:** 2 Tanks · 3 Healers · 5 DPS

**Rule:**
Never let all three Crawler Mines detonate freely. Assign your soakers before the pull — on Heroic each stomp hits for ~1.5M physical.

**Overview:**
Two alternating phases on a roughly 2-minute Assault / 1-minute Siege loop. Assault Phase is the only DPS window. The recommended Siege strategy is to let Shock Pulse knock the entire raid into the far corner, out-ranging Mortar Barrage.

**Lust:**
On pull — the first Assault Phase is the longest uninterrupted DPS window of the fight.

**Heroic Callouts:**
1. Ricochet: spinning sawblade arcs toward two ranged players every ~15 seconds.
2. Mortar Barrage: replaces Mortar Cannon in Siege Phase. Out-range it by letting Shock Pulse knock you to the far corner.
3. Crawler Mine stomps hit ~1.5M physical — soakers need a cooldown every time.

## BOSS 07 — KOR'KRON DARK SHAMAN
**Page:** /bosses/dark-shaman
**Sub:** Haromm and Kardris — Heroic Split Strategy
**Setup:** 3 Tanks · 3 Healers · 4 DPS

**Rule:**
Split the bosses far enough apart that each group only deals with one boss's abilities. Running them together on Heroic is possible but significantly harder.

**Overview:**
Hard split: two tanks and two healers take Haromm to the far north end of the room; one tank, one healer, and all DPS fight Kardris in the main area. Bosses share health. Kill wolves before pushing either boss below 85%.

**Lust:**
Sub-25% — both bosses gain Blood Frenzy. Pop immediately and kill before the damage becomes unmanageable.

**Heroic Callouts:**
1. Hard split is mandatory.
2. Iron Prison fires every 30 seconds. Every DPS and healer needs their own personal cooldown plan.
3. Toxic Mist ramps to 3–400k per tick late in the duration. Paladin Purity on a 30-second cooldown is extremely valuable.
4. Falling Ash hits the entire raid even when split — the Haromm group must be warned on comms.

## BOSS 08 — GENERAL NAZGRIM
**Page:** /bosses/general-nazgrim
**Sub:** Rage Management · Stance Control
**Setup:** 2 Tanks · 2-3 Healers · 5-6 DPS

**Rule:**
Never DPS Nazgrim during Defensive Stance — every hit gives him 3 Rage. His abilities scale with Rage, and a single poorly-timed attack can push him to 100 and spawn a permanent Ravager axe.

**Overview:**
A rage management fight. Nazgrim cycles through three stances in a fixed order: Battle → Berserker → Defensive. The raid's job is to keep his Rage as low as possible by handling Sundering Blow swaps cleanly, destroying Banners fast, and going completely cold during Defensive Stance.

**Lust:**
Just before the 10% burn — ideally entering a Battle or Berserker Stance window with no adds alive.

**Heroic Callouts:**
1. Execute: 3M physical hit on the current tank. Use a minor mitigation cooldown — cannot be dodged, blocked, or parried.
2. Kor'kron Snipers added to add waves. The targeted healer runs behind the Sniper so Multi-Shot fires into the wall.
3. Add waves start at 3 and grow over the fight.

## BOSS 09 — MALKOROK
**Page:** /bosses/malkorok
**Sub:** Ancient Miasma · Orbs · Blood Rage
**Setup:** 2 Tanks · 2 Healers · 6 DPS

**Rule:**
Ancient Miasma replaces ALL healing with shields. Direct heals are wasted. Orbs instantly strip your shield — never touch them.

**Overview:**
Two alternating phases. Phase 1: Ancient Miasma is active — healers build shields, not health. Three Imploding Energy circles must each be soaked by exactly one player. Phase 2: Miasma drops, healing works normally, entire raid stacks in front to split the 6.7M cleave.

**Lust:**
At the start of Phase 2 (Blood Rage transition) — healing works again and the boss takes +25% more damage.

**Heroic Callouts:**
1. Essence of Y'Shaarj orbs persist on the ground throughout Phase 1 — treat them as permanent kill zones.
2. Seismic Slam spawns a Living Corruption add that must be pulled to the center immediately.
3. Hard 6-minute enrage.

## BOSS 10 — SPOILS OF PANDARIA
**Page:** /bosses/spoils-of-pandaria
**Sub:** Two Teams — Two Sides — One Timer
**Setup:** 2 Tanks · 2 Healers · 6 DPS

**Rule:**
Both teams must reach 50 points simultaneously — never let one side get ahead while the other stalls. If the shared 5-minute enrage timer expires before both sides hit 50 points, the encounter resets.

**Overview:**
The raid splits into two equal groups, each clearing one side of a vault simultaneously. Each side has two quadrants — large chests give more points but spawn harder add combinations. Both teams must hit 50 points within the same shared 5-minute timer or it resets.

**Lust:**
No traditional lust window — use when the final quadrant is opened to accelerate the last push.

**Heroic Callouts:**
1. Heroic adds substantially more HP and more dangerous ability combinations.
2. Some Mogu adds on Heroic cast Cryptmaker — a frontal cone that cannot be avoided by the tank. Tanks must coordinate defensive cooldowns.

## BOSS 11 — THOK THE BLOODTHIRSTY
**Page:** /bosses/thok
**Sub:** Healing CD Rotation · Kiting · Cage Management
**Setup:** 2 Tanks · 2 Healers · 6 DPS

**Rule:**
Never let the Screech stack get high enough that healers can't cast. Release a prisoner to reset the stack — but each prisoner adds a new hazard. Manage prisoner releases as tightly as the stacks.

**Overview:**
A kiting fight with an escalating Screech mechanic. Thok gains stacks of Deafening Screech every second — after ~20 stacks healers can't cast and the raid dies. Releasing a prisoner resets the stacks but adds a new environmental hazard. The fight ends when you kill Thok after enough Bloodlust/Frenzy stacks accumulate.

**Lust:**
During the final Frenzy phase when Thok has enough stacks — burst him before the stacks become unmanageable.

**Heroic Callouts:**
1. Heroic adds a Jailer mechanic — a caged add that fixates a random player and must be kited separately from Thok.
2. Ice phases replace Poison phase on Heroic. Ice patches freeze players who walk through them.

## BOSS 12 — SIEGECRAFTER BLACKFUSE
**Page:** /bosses/siegecrafter-blackfuse
**Sub:** Assembly Line + Overcharged Weapons
**Setup:** 2 Tanks · 2 Healers · 6 DPS

**Rule:**
Destroy every weapon before it reaches the end of the assembly line. Overcharged weapons are the enrage — once several weapons reach Blackfuse, the buff stack will outpace your DPS.

**Overview:**
Blackfuse channels weapons off an assembly line. A small team (2-3 DPS) rides the conveyor belt to destroy weapons before they reach him. The ground team handles the boss and add spawns. On Heroic each weapon has a unique sabotage mechanic.

**Lust:**
At the start of the encounter (pull) — maximize the window before the first weapon reaches full speed.

**Heroic Callouts:**
1. Each weapon type has a unique heroic sabotage mechanic — belt team must know all three.
2. Shredder add from a missed weapon has very high HP and deals heavy melee damage.
3. The belt team should be your 2-3 highest sustained DPS — every missed weapon is a wipe-timer accelerant.

## BOSS 13 — PARAGONS OF THE KLAXXI
**Page:** /bosses/paragons-of-the-klaxxi
**Sub:** 9 Paragons — Fixed Heroic Kill Order
**Setup:** 2 Tanks · 2 Healers · 6 DPS

**Rule:**
Follow the fixed Heroic kill order — Rik'kal last is a wipe. Every living Paragon at the end of a kill empowers the next one with a stacking buff.

**Overview:**
9 Paragons are all active simultaneously, each with unique abilities. Killing one empowers all remaining Paragons. The recommended Heroic kill order minimizes overlap of dangerous abilities and prevents Rik'kal from being alive late when his Injection becomes unmanageable.

**Lust:**
On Kil'ruk (the final Paragon) — his Reave makes the fight a race.

**Heroic Callouts:**
1. Fixed kill order is mandatory. Rik'kal alive late = Injection spam = wipe.
2. Kaz'tik's Mesmerize targets 3 random players — run to the designated break zone immediately.
3. Xaril's Toxic Injection applies a powerful DoT — dispel immediately on every application.

## BOSS 14 — GARROSH HELLSCREAM
**Page:** /bosses/garrosh-hellscream
**Sub:** 4-Phase Endgame Fight · The Downfall
**Setup:** 2 Tanks · 2 Healers · 6 DPS

**Rule:**
Never let a Mind Control cast go uninterrupted. Pre-assign CC targets by player name before the pull — one missed cast spreads like a virus and wipes the raid.

**Overview:**
A long, execution-heavy fight across four distinct phases. Phase 1 is add management around Iron Stars. Phase 2 is Desecrate placement, Whirling Corruption positioning, and Mind Control response. Intermissions are puzzle phases inside the Realm of Y'Shaarj. Phase 3 repeats Phase 2 with all abilities empowered. Phase 4 (Heroic only) is entirely new mechanics in Stormwind.

**Lust:**
At the Phase 3 transition (Garrosh hits 10%). All DPS cooldowns should be held for this window.

**Heroic Callouts:**
1. Intermissions are fixed order: Jade Temple → Terrace (skip). Never execute the Terrace — stand at the back wall.
2. Jade Temple: adds cast Embodied Doubt — AoE silence + stacking damage amplifier. Pre-assign interrupt/stun pairs per group of 3 adds.
3. Minions of Y'Shaarj gain 10 energy per melee hit. Kill each one separately — never let two die near each other.
4. Phase 4 (Stormwind) is Heroic-only. Off-tank kites the Iron Star to intercept Manifest Rage. Kill before Manifest Rage fires twice.

**Mechanics:**
1. [🪓] Desecrate — Thrown at a ranged target. Always requires 3 players standing at range to bait it away from the raid. Place weapons along walls — keep the center clear for Phase 3+.
2. [🌀] Whirling Corruption [KEY] — Stack under the boss for normal version. Spread to max range (~40 yards) for Empowered version — a minion spawns where each missile lands. Kill spawned minions before they buff each other.
3. [🧠] Touch of Y'Shaarj — Mind Control [KEY] — MC on 2 players. They spam a cast that chains the MC to others — interrupt or stun every cast immediately. DPS to 20% HP to break. Pre-assign who handles each MC target.
4. [🛡️] Gripping Despair (Tank Swap) — Stacking tank DoT. Swap at 3 stacks. In Phase 3 dropping stacks causes Explosive Despair — tank needs a defensive cooldown.
5. [👹] Realm of Y'Shaarj (Intermission) [KEY] — Kill all adds before Garrosh reaches 25 energy. Heroic: Jade Temple first, Terrace skip strategy.
6. [😈] Manifest Rage (Phase 4) [KEY] — Garrosh casts at 100 energy in Phase 4. Iron Star must hit during the 2-second cast window — not the channel.

**Roles:**
- Tanks: Swap at 3 Gripping Despair stacks. Use a defensive when stacks expire in Phase 3 (Explosive Despair). Phase 4: main tank holds Garrosh; off-tank kites the Iron Star.
- Healers: 2 healers. HPS demand is burst-heavy — disc and paladin are ideal. Save a CD for each Whirling Corruption and the Iron Star explosion in Phase 4.
- DPS: Pre-assign MC targets by name. Desecrate team (3 players) move out every weapon. Phase 4: all DPS stay stacked behind boss for Malice, stepping out on debuff. No DoTs when breaking MC.
